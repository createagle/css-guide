Why the sky turns orange at sunset

By Lin Wei · 4 min read

Sunlight is a mix of every color. On its way to your eyes it bumps into air molecules, and the short blue waves scatter far more than the long red ones.

At noon the light crosses a thin slice of air, so we see the scattered blue. At sunset it crosses forty times more air, the blue is scattered away, and the reds and oranges are what reach us.

The color of the sky is the color of the light that was left over.
