A beginner's guide to sourdough

Sourdough bread rises without commercial yeast. Instead, it relies on a starter: a living mix of flour, water, wild yeast and bacteria that you feed every day.

What you need

  • 500 g bread flour
  • 350 g water
  • 100 g active starter
  • 10 g salt

The long wait

Mix, rest, fold, and let the dough rise slowly overnight in the fridge. The cold slows the yeast down and gives the bacteria time to build flavor.

Bake it in a very hot covered pot for the first twenty minutes so the steam lets the loaf expand.
