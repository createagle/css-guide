The North Ridge in One Day

The North Ridge is the classic long walk of the region: twelve kilometres, almost nine hundred metres of ascent and views over three valleys. Print this page and take it with you; there is no phone signal on the ridge.

Before you go

Check the forecast from the mountain weather service the evening before. The last bus back leaves the village at six.

The route

Follow the red and white stripes from the church. After the bridge, keep left and follow the river; the steep part starts at the old mill and ends at the first cairn.
