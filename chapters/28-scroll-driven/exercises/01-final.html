Walking the Valley

Most walks in the valley start from the village green, where there is free parking and a bus stop.

The river path is flat and good for families. It follows the water for four kilometres to the mill.

From the mill, a steep lane leads up to the ridge. The view from the top takes in three counties on a clear day.

The ridge path is exposed, so bring a windproof layer even in summer.

Coming down, you can choose the short way through the woods or the long way past the reservoir.

Both routes end back at the green, close to the pub and the tea room.
