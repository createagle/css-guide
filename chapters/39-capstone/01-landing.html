Questions

Still unsure? These are the ones we hear most.

Does it really work without signal?

Yes. Downloaded maps, routes and alerts all work offline. GPS does not need a phone signal.

Will it drain my battery?

Recording a six-hour hike typically uses 15 to 20 percent on a recent phone.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes, from your account settings. You keep Explorer until the end of the period.

Where do the routes come from?

Local guides write them and walkers report changes, which we check every week.
