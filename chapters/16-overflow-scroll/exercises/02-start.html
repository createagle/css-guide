Gear Route Food Safety

Gear

Pack light: a waterproof jacket, a warm layer, a map and a headlamp cover most days.

Boots matter more than anything else. Break them in on short walks before a long trip.

A small first-aid kit and a whistle weigh almost nothing and can save a day.

Route

The path is marked with red and white stripes on trees and rocks.

After the bridge, keep left and follow the river for about two kilometres.

The last climb is steep but short; the hut is visible from the top.

Food

Eat a little every hour instead of one big lunch.

Nuts, dried fruit and bread keep well and give steady energy.

Refill your water at the spring below the hut; it is safe to drink.

Safety

Check the forecast the evening before and again in the morning.

Tell someone your route and when you expect to be back.

If the weather turns, turning back is always the right choice.
