Ada Lin

Ada Lin is a type designer and letterpress printer. She spent ten years drawing signage alphabets for railway stations before setting up her own small studio by the harbour, where she still prints a small book of poems every winter.

Her work mixes old and new: metal type and hand-set pages on one side, variable fonts and web typography on the other. She teaches a weekend course on both, and likes to say that a good page is mostly about the white space.
